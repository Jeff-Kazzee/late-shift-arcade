# Wayfinder map — a playable arcade with creator submissions

Label: `wayfinder:map`

## Destination

A decision-complete product plan for a polished browser arcade where players can
play complete games and external creators can submit games through a safe, curated publication path.

## Notes

- Canonical product direction: `product-specs/ARCADE-PLATFORM-PRD.md`
- Use finite appetites and player/operator proof.
- Preserve the Legacy Rack.
- One primary agent by default.
- Planning may define decisions; implementation happens in later bounded bets.

## Decisions so far

- [Separate truth, assignments, and game design](../../decisions/0002-reset-game-development-process.md) — Vivary owns truth, Relay owns handoffs, and GDDs own player experience.

## Not yet specified

- Player identity and cross-device score ownership.
- Creator accounts and public dashboards.
- Community-game hosting provider and moderation implementation.
- Ratings, comments, discovery ranking, and economic model.

## Out of scope

- A 30-game commitment before the submission loop is proven.
- Automatic publication.
- Multiplayer, payments, and revenue sharing in the first MVP.
- Completing DEEPSHIFT's full future vision as a prerequisite for the arcade.
